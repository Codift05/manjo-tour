## Ringkasan Progres per Fase

### Phase 1: Setup & Design System (Selesai)
- Audit kontras WCAG AA seluruh palet brand (`#2ECC71`, `#FF7A3D`, `#E8F8F5`, `#FEF3C7`, `#1E293B`, `#64748B`, dan High-Contrast Riding Mode `#0F172A` / `#4ADE80`).
- Inisialisasi proyek Flutter `touring_app` (`lib/`, Android, iOS, Web, Linux) dengan `google_fonts` (Plus Jakarta Sans) dan `lottie`.
- Implementasi tema, token warna/spasi/gerak, komponen dasar (`OrganicCard`, `TactileButton`, `FloatingNavBar`, `ScenicIllustration`, `MascotArt`, `StateViews`), model data, dan `TouringController`.

### Phase 2: Core Screens & Navigation Flow (Selesai)
- **Home / Explore** (`home_screen.dart`, `convoy_action_modals.dart`, `route_detail_sheet.dart`): Header personal, Riding Mode toggle, Notifikasi, Hero Banner Panda Rider, CTA "Mulai Konvoi Baru" & "Gabung Kode Rute", Search Bar, Category Pills, Carousel Rute Populer, dan Detail Rute dengan unduh peta offline lokal.
- **Trips & Split-Bill** (`trips_screen.dart`, `trip_detail_screen.dart`): Kartu perjalanan, filter status, simulasi state loading/error/empty, checkpoint timeline, peserta konvoi, dan patungan biaya otomatis (bensin, makan, penginapan) dengan toggle Lunas / Belum Bayar.
- **Profile & Digital Patches** (`profile_screen.dart`, `patch_detail_dialog.dart`, `network_performance_sheet.dart`): Header lanskap sage, arc level rider, 6 lencana Digital Patch kustom, Leaderboard podium 3-tangga, dan inspector Performa & Load Balancer.

### Phase 3: Interactive Convoy Map & Safety Overlays (Selesai)
- **Live Convoy Map** (`convoy_radar_screen.dart`, `convoy_map_canvas.dart`): Peta vektor terang & High-Contrast Riding Mode, animasi pergerakan marker anggota (`Leader`, `Sweeper`, `You`), Lottie radar pulse pada marker aktif, Gap Alert Banner otomatis (> 500m), dan kontrol zoom.
- **Safety & Standalone Comms**: Push-to-Talk 64px dengan animasi Panda HT + Lottie soundwave (`walkie_talkie_overlay.dart`), Quick-Tap Hazard Pinning 4 kategori (`hazard_pin_sheet.dart`), dan Crash Detection 10s Countdown Overlay (`crash_sos_overlay.dart`).

### Phase 4: Polish, Micro-Animations & Verifikasi (Selesai)
- Shell responsif (`app_shell.dart`) dengan Floating Bottom Nav Bar di mobile dan `NavigationRail` pada layar lebar (`>= 900px`), transisi `180ms` ease-out, haptic feedback, dan dukungan `disableAnimations`.
- Zero overflow terverifikasi di breakpoint 360px, 390px, dan 1024px.
